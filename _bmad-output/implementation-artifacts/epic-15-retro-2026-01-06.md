## What Went Well

### Detailed Comparison

The comparison system precisely identifies the differences between two executions.

**Impact:** Fine-grained understanding of variations, easier debugging

### Impact Analysis

Impact analysis makes it possible to evaluate the effect of changes before and after deployment.

**Impact:** Informed decisions, reduced risk

### Advanced Filtering

Advanced filtering with multiple operators is very powerful and flexible.

**Impact:** Targeted analyses, precise data extraction

---

## Challenges Encountered

### Comparison Complexity

Comparing two executions in detail can be complex, especially with many events.

**Solution:** Optimized diff algorithm, logical grouping

### Filtering Performance

Advanced filtering with many criteria can be slow.

**Solution:** Optimized evaluation, indexing of frequent fields

### Impact Metrics

Defining the right metrics for impact analysis required iterations.

**Solution:** Standardized, extensible metrics

---

## Learnings

1. **Comparison:** Good comparison requires a clear data structure
2. **Metrics:** Impact metrics must be relevant and actionable
3. **Filtering:** Advanced filtering is powerful but must be performant
