## What Went Well

### Approval System

The `ApprovalManager` works perfectly. It allows critical actions to be paused and to wait for human approval.

**Impact:** Enhanced security, granular control

### Multi-Dimensional Budgets

The complex budget system enables very fine-grained control of costs and usage.

**Impact:** Cost optimization, precise control

### Condition Evaluator

The condition evaluator is powerful and flexible, enabling very sophisticated policies.

**Impact:** Expressive policies, suited to complex cases

### Audit Trail

The complete audit trail makes it possible to trace every policy check.

**Impact:** Full traceability, compliance

---

## Challenges Encountered

### Complexity of Conditions

Conditional expressions can become complex. The evaluator had to be well structured.

**Solution:** Hierarchical structure with clear logical operators

### Budget Performance

Real-time budget tracking can impact performance.

**Solution:** Query optimization, computation caching

### Approval Management

Managing the state of pending approvals requires particular attention.

**Solution:** Clear state machine, configurable timeout

---

## Learnings

1. **Extensibility:** The base policy system was well designed for extension
2. **Performance:** Budgets require particular attention to performance
3. **UX:** Human approval must be simple and clear for users
